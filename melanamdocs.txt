Melanam: Meetings, Recordings, and AI Notes

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Melanam is a video meeting app. It helps people start or join private meeting rooms, collaborate with room tools, and return to recordings and AI-generated notes afterward.

GETTING STARTED
1. Open the Meetings workspace and sign in or create an account.
2. Use a modern browser and allow camera/microphone access when prompted.
3. Check Pricing for current plan and feature availability.

START OR JOIN A MEETING
- Start meeting opens an instant room.
- Create meeting lets you enter a title and optional agenda before opening a room.
- Join meeting accepts a room code or complete invite link.
- Recent meeting cards let you reopen a room or copy its invite link.
- Share private invite links only with intended participants.

ROOM TOOLS
- Copy link copies the invite link for the current room.
- Record starts or stops server recording. Only the host can control recording; availability may depend on the account plan.
- Whiteboard opens the shared whiteboard.
- Files opens the file sharing panel; access may require an active plan.
- Live captions may require Pro.
- The available controls depend on the user's role and plan. Tell attendees when a meeting is recorded or transcribed.

RECORDINGS
Open /meetings/recordings to see recordings available to your account. A recording appears after the host records and ends the meeting and processing finishes. Ready recordings can be watched or downloaded. Hosts can share recordings with the meeting group or delete their recording. Processing, expired, or deleted recordings may not be available.

MEETING NOTES
Open /meetings/notes to review processed meeting summaries, key points, decisions, action items, and transcript lines. Processing starts after the meeting and may take a few minutes. Download the transcript when transcript lines are available. Review AI-generated notes for accuracy before sharing.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- For camera or microphone issues, check browser permissions, device selection, and whether another app is using the device.
- If others cannot hear you, check that your microphone is not muted.
- For choppy audio or video, use a stronger connection or turn off video.
- For joining problems, check that the invite link is complete and that you are signed in to the right account.
- If a recording or notes are missing, wait for processing to finish and check again.
