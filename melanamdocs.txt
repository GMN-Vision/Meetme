PART 1: ABOUT US
 Meet. Teach. Remember.
Melanam is a secure workspace for live video meetings, online learning, and the intelligence that carries work forward after the call ends.

Most video tools stop at "End meeting." The recording lands in one place, the notes in another, the files somewhere else, and the follow-up tasks get lost. Melanam was built to close that gap. Every session, whether a live class, a team call, or a seminar, becomes one connected record: the video, the transcript, the AI-generated summary, the shared files, the decisions, and the next actions.

 What we do
Melanam combines three things that are usually separate products:

- Video meetings: private rooms with invite links, recording, livestreaming, live captions, whiteboards, and polls.
- Learning management (LMS): role-aware courses, sessions, assignments, and resources for instructors and students.
- AI meeting memory: transcripts, summaries, decisions, and action items captured automatically after every session.

Our principles
One record for every session. Nothing that happens live should disappear. Recordings, transcripts, files, and follow-ups stay together and stay connected to the course or project they belong to.

Private by design. Rooms are private, workspaces are controlled, and access is role-aware. People see what their role allows, nothing more.

Intelligent by default. AI is built into the workflow instead of added on top. Summaries and action items appear without anyone having to take notes.

Work continues after the call. A session is a starting point. Melanam helps you return to the lesson outline, the assigned task, or the decision that was made.

 Who Melanam is for
- Educators and institutions running live classes, cohorts, and courses
- Trainers and coaches delivering seminars and workshops
- Teams that want meetings to produce clear outcomes
- Organisations that need controlled, role-based access to their sessions

 4. Meeting Rooms
 Start a meeting
1. Open your workspace and select New room (or start a session from a course).
2. Name the room and set any options such as recording or captions.
3. Select Start.
Invite participants
Each room has a private invite link. Copy it and share it by email, chat, or calendar invite. Only people with the link (and, where applicable, the right workspace role) can join.
 Join a meeting
1. Open the invite link.
2. Sign in if prompted.
3. Check your camera and microphone, then select Join.
 Leave or end a session
Participants can leave at any time. A host or instructor can select End session, which closes the room and begins processing the recording, transcript, and AI summary.

 7. AI Meeting Memory
After a session ends, Melanam produces a structured record automatically.
Output  What you get 
Transcript  A written record of what was said. 
 Summary  A concise overview of the session. 
 Decisions  Key decisions made during the meeting. 
 Action items  Tasks and next steps that came out of the discussion. 
 Shared resources  Files and links attached to the session. 

During a live session, you may also see a Live summary panel that captures key points and resources as they come up.
 Tips for better results
- Use a good microphone and reduce background noise.
- Speak one at a time where possible.
- State decisions and tasks explicitly (for example, "Action: Priya will send the draft by Friday").
- Review AI outputs before sharing them. Summaries are helpful, but should be checked for accuracy.
Participation history
After a session, review who attended and engagement context alongside the recording, which helps instructors follow up with learners.
 8. Learning Workspaces (LMS)
The learning workspace brings courses and live sessions together.
- Courses: organize learning into sessions, modules, and materials.
- Sessions: schedule or launch live classes directly from a course.
- Assignments: create, distribute, and track student work.
- Resources: attach readings, slides, and files to a course or session.
- Role-aware views: instructors, students, and administrators each see the tools relevant to them.
 Typical teaching workflow
1. Create a course and add its first session.
2. Collaborate live using video, captions, whiteboard, polls, and files.
3. Capture the recording, transcript, and AI summary automatically.
4. Continue by assigning follow-up work and pointing learners to session resources.
 Typical learner workflow

1. Open the course from your workspace.
2. Join the live session from the course page.
3. After class, revisit the recording, summary, and resources.
4. Complete assignments and follow action items.

 13. Troubleshooting

 Problem  Try this 

 Can't hear or see others  Check your device volume and output selection. Refresh the page and rejoin. 
 Others can't hear me  Confirm the microphone isn't muted and that the browser has microphone permission. 
 Camera not detected  Close other apps using the camera, then allow camera access in your browser settings. 
 Choppy video or audio  Switch to a stronger connection, turn off your video, or close bandwidth-heavy apps. 
 Can't join a room  Confirm the invite link is complete and that you're signed in with the correct account. 
 Captions not appearing  Make sure captions are enabled for the session and that audio is clear. 
 Summary or transcript missing  Processing begins after the session ends and can take a few minutes. Check again shortly. 
If a problem persists, check the [Status](https://www.melanam.com/status) page for known issues.
